# Biology Question Writer

This Chainlit chatbot is designed to assist with creating study questions for the New York State Regents Biology exam. 

The app can engage in conversations about designing general review and cluster questions.

## General Review

General review questions help students recall and reinforce foundational biology concepts across the major topic of the NYS Biology curriculum (Structure and Function, Matter and Energy in Organisms and Ecosystems, Interdependent Relationships in Ecosystems, Inheritance and Variation of Traits, and Natural Selection and Evolution).

## Cluster Questions

Cluster questions are sets of multiple choice and constructed response questions accompanied by reading passages, charts, and/or images. A cluster is focused on a specific real-world pheomenon. These questions are meant to imitate the ones seen in the New York State Regents Biology exam.

Note: This Chainlit app currently does not generate images, but it can provide a description of a suitable image to include in the cluster. Similarly, the app presents the underlying data table of a graph instead of visualizing the graph itself.

## LLM Provider

The chatbot can be configured to use a model from Anthropic or OpenAI.
